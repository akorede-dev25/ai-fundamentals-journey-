# What I learned: the complexity of data

![Graphic](IMG_NAME.png)

## What is data?
Information that people collect, store, and use for analysis, decision making, or communication in digital systems. It underpins every AI system.

## Types of data
- **Structured:** fits neatly into rows and columns
- **Semi-structured:** some order, but not a strict table
- **Unstructured:** text, images, audio, video

## Why some data is harder to work with
- **Inconsistency:** no predictable format, like customer feedback in different formats or languages
- **Variability:** many forms, each needing different processing and storage

## Why AI matters here
Traditional data tools weren't built for this complexity. AI helps find patterns and meaning in data that has no set format.
