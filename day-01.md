# Day 1: What is AI?

## What I learned
- AI is the ability of machines to perform tasks that usually need human intelligence, like recognizing patterns, making decisions, and understanding language.
- AI learns from data. Better data gives better results.

## The 4 forms of AI
- **Generative AI:** creates content
- **Predictive AI:** forecasts what's next
- **Decision-making AI:** chooses actions
- **Computer vision AI:** understands images

## Line that stuck with me
"AI provides the speed, you provide the direction."
